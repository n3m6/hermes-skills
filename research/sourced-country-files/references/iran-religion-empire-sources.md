## Mechanism numbers worth reusing (all from verified pages)

- Tobacco: concession 20 Mar 1890 (Talbot, £15k/yr + 25% of profits); fatwa Dec 1891 ("war against the Imam of the Age"); cancelled 26 Jan 1892; £500k compensation. British "waffling in support" of the company by Jan 1892.
- 1906: 12,000–14,000 merchants in British Legation *bast*; ~12,000 in the gardens ("vast open-air school of political science").
- 1907 Convention: signed 31 Aug 1907; Russian north / British SE / neutral middle (oil-bearing SW in neutral zone).
- Shuster affair: Treasurer-General May–Dec 1911; expelled under joint Russo-British pressure; Russia occupied Tabriz/north.
- D'Arcy 1901: £20,000 + 16% of profits, 60 yrs, ~480k sq mi; oil struck 26 May 1908 at Masjed Soleyman; APOC 1909; **Admiralty bought 51% in 1914 (Churchill)**; Nov 1932 Reza cancelled → PCIJ/League → 1933 agreement (to 1993, area −75%, 4s/ton, 20% of distributable profits above minimum, £750k floor).
- 1919 agreement: concluded 9 Aug 1919 (Cox–Vosuq letters); denounced by Majlis 22 Jun 1921; full text NOT located online.
- 1921 coup: 22 Feb 1921; Ironside appointed Reza Khan Cossack commander Dec 1920, "groomed him... leaving instructions for Reza to be gradually released from British control" (Ghani 2001 pp. 146–148, 153–155); War Office report 8 Dec 1920 ("depart in peace and honour", Ullman 1972 vol 3 p. 384).
- Secularization: Qom shrine incident Mar 1928; Western dress law Dec 1928; Goharshad Mosque suppression Jul 1935 (100–500 dead); **Kashf-e hijab 8 Jan 1936**; madrasa enrollments 5,984→785 (1925–41).
- 1941: Operation Countenance 25 Aug 1941; abdication 16 Sep 1941; Reza exiled as British prisoner (Mauritius → South Africa, d. 26 Jul 1944); British preferred restoring Qajars.
- 1951–53: nationalization Mar 1951; production 664k→27k bbl (1950→52); £10,000/month via Woodhouse to Rashidian brothers (buying "religious leaders" per CIA estimates, Kinzer pp. 150–151); $1m approved 4 Apr 1953 (Dulles); 19 Aug 1953 coup; Mosaddegh sentenced 21 Dec 1953.
