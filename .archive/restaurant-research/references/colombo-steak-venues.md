# Colombo (Sri Lanka) — Steak-with-view venues, verified 30 Aug 2026

All dishes verified against official menu PDFs (dates noted). Prices LKR, +10% service + taxes typically.

## Tier 1 — literal pepper steak + oceanfront setting

### Kings Steakbar — The Kingsbury (Colombo 01)
- 48 Janadhipathi Mawatha, Colombo 01 · +94 112 421 221 · Dinner 7:00–11:00 pm, Sunday carvery 11:30 am–3:30 pm
- **Dish: "Pepper Steak Flambé" 200g — LKR 14,000** (tableside flambé) — the only Colombo venue with a literal beef pepper steak. **Green Peppercorn Sauce** available on all steaks; full lineup: Australian/US Angus, Wagyu (Kobe, Ōmi), Tomahawk LKR 45,000, Chateaubriand, Beef Wellington (LKR 32,000 each).
- View: oceanfront hotel directly opposite Galle Face Green (harbour + ocean). Caveat: Steakbar room is an interior fine-dining room (live piano/cello), NOT a panorama window. Rooftop views = sibling **Sky Lounge** (cocktails/tapas only).
- Menu PDF: https://thekingsburyhotel.eme-devops.com/2026/01/Kingsteakbar-New-Menu-14.pdf (Jan 2026)

### 1864 Limited Edition — Galle Face Hotel (Colombo 03)
- 2 Galle Road, Colombo 3 · +94 11 754 1010 · Dinner 7:00–10:30 pm, casual elegant
- Dish: Australian Rib-Eye **LKR 14,300** / Grilled Sirloin **LKR 9,340** + **Black Peppercorn Sauce** (+LKR 640) — on both lunch and seasonal menus.
- View: iconic oceanfront heritage hotel on Galle Face Green. Best sunset = hotel's **Chequerboard terrace**; 1864 is the chic indoor fine-dining room (sea-facing table on request).
- Menu PDFs: https://gallefacehotel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/1864-LUNCH-MENU-2025.pdf · .../uploads/2026/08/Seasonal-menu.pdf

### Virticle by Jetwing — Jetwing Colombo Seven (Colombo 07)
- True rooftop restaurant + bar, panoramic 360° city skyline views, evening live music.
- Dish: Australian Angus **Ribeye 250g LKR 12,300** / **Tenderloin 225g LKR 11,800**; sauces incl. **Pepper jus** (also mushroom, tamarind red wine, lemon butter, teriyaki truffle).
- Menu: https://www.virticlebyjetwing.com/restaurant-menu

### Governor's Restaurant — Mount Lavinia Hotel (beachfront, ~9 km south of Fort)
- 100 Hotel Road, Mount Lavinia · +94 11 2 711711
- Dish: Black Angus Tenderloin (180g), Cape Grim Striploin (200g), Rangers Valley Wagyu Striploin (200g), sauces incl. **Pepper Jus**.
- ⚠️ Verified from **Aug 2019 menu** (site 503 as of Aug 2026) — call to confirm current menu. Legendary ocean-view terrace directly over the beach.
- Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20190923203831id_/https://www.mountlaviniahotel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/governors-menu-aug-19.pdf

## Tier 2 — steak yes, view limited

### Capital Bar & Grill — Shangri-La Colombo (Colombo 02)
- 1 Galle Face, Colombo 02 — **Level 2, NO elevated view** (despite "Capital" name). Stylish grill room, oceanfront hotel.
- Dish: Tenderloin/Ribeye/Striploin 180/240/350g **LKR 9,900–25,900**; **Black Pepper Mushroom** topping (also herb jus, bourbon BBQ, béarnaise); Tajima Wagyu MB6+, Icon Wagyu MB9+.
- Menu PDF: https://sitecore-cd.shangri-la.com/-/media/Shangri-La/colombo_shangrila/dining/restaurants-bars-lounges/capital-bar-and-grill/03102025-capital-bar-grill-food-menu.pdf (03 Oct 2025)

## Context
- Rooftops with views but cocktails/tapas only (NOT steak dinners): Sky Lounge (Kingsbury), ON14 (OZO, ocean view), Cloud Red (Cinnamon Red).
- Proper pepper steaks but NO view (excluded per "views" brief): London Grill (Cinnamon Grand — basement), Steak & Grill (Taj Samudra — interior).
- Local-style "beef pepper steak" (beef in peppery gravy): Green Cabin, Upali's — cheap, no views.
- Sri Lankan restaurant price notes: ~10% service charge + government taxes on top; hotel menus change seasonally.
