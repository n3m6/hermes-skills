## Key verified facts & dates
- San Remo mandate award: 25 Apr 1920; draft mandate Jun/Dec 1920; NEVER ratified; superseded by 1922 treaty. Class A, Art. 22.
- 1920 revolt: May–Oct 1920; al-Shirazi fatwas (service in British admin unlawful; defensive force permitted); Najaf burial-tax grievance (Wadi al-Salam); RAF: 4,008 hrs, 97 tons bombs; cost £40–50m; 400–1,000 British dead, 2,050–10,000 Iraqis.
- Cairo Conference: 12–30 Mar 1921; Faisal chosen; RAF air control decision.
- Referendum: 16 Jul–11 Aug 1921, announced 19 Aug, 96% for Faisal; coronation 23 Aug 1921 (Eid al-Ghadir, 18 Dhu'l-Hijja).
- Anglo-Iraqi Treaty: signed 10 Oct 1922 (Cox + Naqib Abd al-Rahman al-Gillani); ratified 1924 under Dobbs' pressure; Cmd 2370 [unverified].
- al-Khalisi: 1922 election-boycott fatwa; exile 1923 (Bombay attempt → Aden → Hajj → Iran); died Mashhad 1925.
- Cox direct rule interlude: Aug–Sep 1922 during Faisal's appendicitis.
- Assyrian Levies: 1919 origins; 1921 Cairo mission; Jul 1922 no more Arabs; by 1928 wholly Assyrian; Colonial Office→Air Ministry Jul 1928; 1 Jun 1932 memorial (refusal to serve); 1941 Habbaniya (6 companies); 11,000 recruited post-1941; RAF Levies 1943/44; disbanded 2 May 1955.
- Simele: Aug 1933; Bakr Sidqi; British estimate ~600 (Sykes 550), Assyrian 3,000–6,000; Mar Shimun detained Jun 1933, exiled to Cyprus on RAF plane 18 Aug 1933; Britain rejected international inquiry.
- 1930 treaty: 30 Jun 1930; in force 3 Oct 1932 (League admission); 25 years.
- 1941: Mufti in Baghdad from 16 Oct 1939; ID 18,000 subvention + ID 1,000/mo; coup 1 Apr 1941 (Golden Square: Sabbagh, Shabib, Said, Salman); war 2–31 May 1941; Mufti declared jihad 2 May; Habbaniya siege; Abd al-Ilah restored 1 Jun; Nuri PM 10 Oct 1941; occupation until late 1947; Farhud 1–2 Jun 1941.
