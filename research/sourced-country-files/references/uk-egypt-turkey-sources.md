## Verified figure dates (Wikipedia wikitext infoboxes/sections, 2 Sep 2026)

- Cromer: Agent & Consul-General 11 Sep 1883 – 6 May 1907; Denshawai incident 1906 (four hanged); *Modern Egypt* 1908.
- Kitchener: Sirdar Apr 1892; Atbara Apr 1898, Omdurman 2 Sep 1898; Mahdi's tomb blown up (Queen Victoria protested); Gov.-Gen. Sudan Sep 1898; Consul-General 12 Jul 1911 – 5 Aug 1914; Earl 29 Jun 1914.
- Gorst: Consul-General 16 May 1907 – 12 Jul 1911 (died in office); Boutros Ghali appointment 1908–09; Press Law 1909; Suez concession attempt 1909–10.
- Wingate: Sudan Gov.-Gen. 22 Dec 1899 – 31 Dec 1916; HC Egypt Jan 1917 – Oct 1919.
- Allenby: HC Oct 1919 – Jun 1925 (resigned May 1925); 1922 declaration (28 Feb) + four reserved points; post-Stack ultimatum Nov 1924 (£500,000 fine, Egyptian withdrawal from Sudan); Stack shot 19 Nov, died 20 Nov 1924.
- Lloyd: HC 1925–29, resignation forced by Labour's Henderson; *Egypt Since Cromer* 1933–34; London Central Mosque memo to Churchill c.1940 ("more Moslems than Christians").
- Lampson: HC 1934–36 → Ambassador 1936–46 (predecessor Percy Loraine); Abdeen Palace 4 Feb 1942 ultimatum; 1935 veto of Farouk's Turin education.
- Stack: Sirdar 1916–24 & Gov.-Gen. 1917–24; killed 19/20 Nov 1924.
- Turkey chain: Calthorpe Mudros 30 Oct 1918, fleet in Constantinople 13 Nov 1918, HC to Aug 1919; Harington GOC occupation forces 1920–23, Chanak Sept 1922, Mehmed VI on HMS Malaya 17 Nov 1922; Rumbold HC Constantinople Nov 1920–23/24, Lausanne delegate Nov 1922–Jul 1923; Mallet ambassador 1913 – Oct 1914, dreadnought seizure Aug 1914.

## Identity traps (recorded so they aren't re-cited)

- Sir Horace Rumbold (1829–1913, father) vs Sir Horace Rumbold (1869–1941, son): archive.org "Recollections of a Diplomatist" memoirs belong to the FATHER.
- Sir Louis Mallet (1823–1890, elder) vs Sir Louis du Pan Mallet (1864–1936, ambassador to the Porte): "Life and Letters of Sir Louis Mallet" concerns the elder.
- Eldon Gorst (1861–1911) is the SON of Sir John Eldon Gorst (Solicitor-General) — born "John Lowndes Gorst".
