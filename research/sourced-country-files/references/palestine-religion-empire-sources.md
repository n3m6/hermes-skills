## The policy chain (Cmd numbers + dates — keep consistent)

1852 firman (status quo) → Mandate Arts 13–16 (1922) → 1928 Yom Kippur mechitza incident →
status quo White Paper 11 Dec 1928 → July 1929 worship rules → riots 23–29 Aug 1929 (133 Jews
killed, 339 injured; 116 Arabs killed, ≥232 wounded) → Shaw Cmd 3530 (Mar 1930) → Wailing Wall
Commission (Dec 1930) → Hope Simpson Cmd 3686 (Oct 1930) → Passfield Cmd 3692 (20 Oct 1930) →
MacDonald letter to Weizmann (13 Feb 1931, reversal) → 1936–39 revolt → Peel Cmd 5479 (Jul 1937,
partition) → Woodhead Cmd 5854 (1938, rejects partition) → 1939 White Paper Cmd 6019 → AAC report
(Apr 1946, 100,000 certificates) → UNGA 181 (29 Nov 1947) → Mandate ends 15 May 1948.

## Key structural facts (verified)

- Office of "Grand Mufti" created by Ronald Storrs in 1918 (first holder Kamil al-Husayni);
  title enlarged for Amin al-Husseini in 1921–22.
- Amin al-Husseini: sentenced in absentia 10 yrs (1920 Nebi Musa riots), excluded from Samuel's
  Jul 1920 amnesty, pardoned 1921; placed LAST in the 1921 mufti election yet appointed by Samuel
  (Nashashibi front-runner induced to withdraw — mayoralty/muftiship clan-balancing); elected SMC
  president 9 Jan 1922 (40/47); deposed 1 Oct 1937; fled Haram 14 Oct 1937; Berlin Nov 1941–1945;
  extradition requested 24 May 1945, refused by France.
- SMC: Dec 1921 Samuel regulation; £50,000 budget; waqf/orphan funds + sharia courts + teacher/
  preacher appointments; secret British subventions from 1931 (Khalidi); 1926 election annulled by
  High Court; reconfigured Oct 1937 (appointments subject to High Commissioner's approval);
  dissolved by Jordan Jan 1951.
- Chief Rabbinate: Rabbinical Assembly 24 Feb 1921 (Kook Ashkenazi, Jacob Meir Sephardi);
  Government recognition in Palestine Gazette No. 40 (1 Apr 1921); replaced Ottoman Hakham Bashi.
- Agudat Yisrael: founded Kattowitz 1912; anti-Zionist in Palestine; Jacob de Haan assassinated
  by Haganah 1924; 1933 agreement = 6.5% of immigration permits.
