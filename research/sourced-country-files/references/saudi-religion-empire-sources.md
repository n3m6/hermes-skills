## Treaties (primary)

- **Treaty of Jeddah 1927 — full text, Cmd. 2951, Treaty Series No. 25** `[OK]` downloaded + `pdftotext`-extracted:
  http://treaties.fcdo.gov.uk/data/Library2/pdf/1927-TS0025.pdf
  - Signed Jeddah 20 May 1927 by Sir Gilbert Clayton & Amir Faisal; ratifications exchanged 17 Sept 1927. Art. 1: Britain recognizes "complete and absolute independence" of Hejaz & Nejd. Art. 3: hajj facilitated to British subjects/protected persons of the Moslem faith. Art. 4: deceased pilgrims' property → British Agent in Jeddah. Art. 6: peaceful relations with Kuwait/Bahrain/Qatar/Oman Coast. Art. 7: slave-trade cooperation. Art. 9: voids the 26 Dec 1915 treaty on ratification. Exchanged notes (19–21 May 1927): Hejaz–Transjordan frontier, Ma'an–Aqaba status quo, British manumission right retained.
- **Aitchison, C.U., A Collection of Treaties, Engagements and Sanads, Vol. XI** (5th ed., Delhi, 1933) `[OK]` full OCR text via archive.org download (filename has spaces — URL-encode):
  https://archive.org/details/acollectionoftreatiesengagementsandsanadsaitchisonc.u.vol11_83_O
  - Treaties numbered per vol: Darin = Treaty No. II (Dec 1915, at Darin/Tarut: Britain acknowledged his independence + aid in certain contingencies; Bin Saud agreed not to alienate territory; annulled by Treaty of Jeddah, No. VII). 1922 Mohammerah Agreement (No. III) + Uqair protocols (Dec 1922); Kuwait boundary (No. IV, Uqair Dec 1922); 1923 Eastern & General Syndicate (London) Hasa oil concession — lapsed; Nov 1926 Bahra (No. V, Iraq–Nejd raids) + Hadda (No. VI, Nejd–Transjordan); May 1927 Jeddah (No. VII, full text matches FCDO PDF).
  - Narrative gold: 1903 first Saudi overtures to Resident at Bushire declined ("absolute aloofness from the affairs of Nejd"); 1906 overtures refused; 1913 Saud drives Turks from Hasa; 1914 Ottoman settlement aborted by war; Shakespear killed "within a month of his arrival"; Kuwait meeting Nov 1916; Ha'il capitulated Nov 1921; Khurma/Turba 1919; Taif & Mecca 1924; full Hejaz by 1926; 1928 Busaiyah police-post dispute → Riyadh conference → 1929 rebellion → suppression → Feb 1930 Faisal–Bin Saud meeting on HMS Lupin.
- **Hurewitz, J.C., Diplomacy in the Near and Middle East, vol. 2** (Princeton, 1956) `[META]` lending-restricted: https://archive.org/details/diplomacyinnearm0002hure — holds the full Darin 1915 text (not printed in Aitchison's OCR).
