## Source quirks (Colombo buffet research)
- **Pulse.lk roundups** (`pulse.lk/everythingelse/colombo-buffet-roundup-<year>/`) — annual snapshots (Feb 2024, Apr 2025), most complete public list. curl-friendly.
- **Happening Colombo** (`happeningcolombo.com/offer-by-type/lunch`) — rolling listings, generally current (matched Amari's Aug 2026 hotel FB price). curl-friendly, text extraction easy.
- **Hotel Facebook/Instagram posts are often the most current price source** — official pages sometimes omit buffet prices entirely (Sheraton, Amari, NH Collection, Mount Lavinia, Courtyard all confirmed via their own social posts).
- **Mövenpick Colombo rebranded to NH Collection Colombo** (~2024) — AYU all-day dining retained; don't search the old brand.
- **Recurring bank-card buffet discounts** (20–25% off food): Nations Trust/Amex via americanexpress.lk offers, People's Bank, mypromo.lk listings — worth checking before quoting full price.
- **ITC Ratnadipa** — itcratnadipa.com DNS-failed from research box; use Marriott Luxury Collection page (cmblc) or itchotels.com instead.
- Official sites that list prices (Shangri-La, Cinnamon Hotels) beat third-party roundups; when official site hours contradict roundups (Nuga Gama: dinner-only listed vs weekend village lunch claims), flag it and give the reservation line.
