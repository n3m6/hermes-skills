## Primary sources — verified full texts

- **Sykes–Picot Agreement (16 May 1916)** — https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sykes–Picot_Agreement
  Verified quote: France and Britain "are prepared to recognize and protect an independent Arab state or a confederation of Arab states... under the suzerainty of an Arab chief"; blue area = French direct control, red = British, brown = international administration (Jerusalem); interior Syria (Damascus/Homs/Hama/Aleppo) falls in French sphere (area a). Note en-dash in URL.
- **McMahon–Hussein Correspondence (14 Jul 1915 – 10 Mar 1916)** — https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/McMahon–Hussein_Correspondence
  Verified quote (McMahon letter of 24 Oct 1915): "The two districts of Mersina and Alexandretta and portions of Syria lying to the west of the districts of Damascus, Homs, Hama and Aleppo cannot be said to be purely Arab, and should be excluded from the limits demanded." Backup PDF `[search-verified]`: https://www1.udel.edu/History-old/figal/Hist104/assets/pdf/readings/13mcmahonhussein.pdf
- **Resolutions of the General Syrian Congress (Damascus, 2 Jul 1919)** — https://bcc-cuny.digication.com/MWHreader/Resolutions_of_the_General_Syrian_Congress_1919
  Verified clauses: (1) full absolute independence; (2) constitutional monarchy, Faisal as king; (3) protest League Covenant Art. XXII tutelage; (4) US technical/economic assistance max 20 yrs; (5) alternatively Great Britain, same cap; (6) "We do not recognize to the French Government any right to any part of Syria"; (7) reject Zionist claims / Jewish commonwealth in southern Syria (Palestine), oppose Jewish immigration; (8) no dismemberment (no separation of Palestine, coast, or Lebanon); (10) protest secret treaties (Sykes-Picot).
- **King–Crane Commission Report (28 Aug 1919)** — https://wwi.lib.byu.edu/index.php/The_King-Crane_Report
  Full text (BYU WWI Document Archive; Wikisource does NOT have it). Verified recommendations: (1) mandatory "not at all as a colonizing Power... but as a Mandatary under the League of Nations"; (2) "the unity of Syria be preserved"; (3) Syria under one Mandatary Power; (4) "Emir Feisal be made head of the new united Syrian State"; (5) "serious modification of the extreme Zionist program". Verified on France: "if America cannot take the mandate for all Syria, that it be given to Great Britain... the longer the Commission remained in Syria, the more clear it became that that course [France] could not be taken." Petition stats: French mandate first choice 14.52%, American 60%, against Zionist program 72.3%. Archive.org mirror of hri.org index: https://web.archive.org/web/20230904094019/http://www.hri.org/docs/king-crane/ `[search-verified]`

## Honest framing notes for the deliverable

- The mandate in Syria was **French** (League of Nations mandate, San Remo Apr 1920; French occupation Jul 1920 after Maysalun). The **British dimension runs through**: WWI promises (McMahon–Hussein), the Sharifian solution (Faisal as king, per King-Crane and the Damascus Congress), Faisal–Weizmann (British-linked), the 1919–20 Anglo-French rivalry at Paris/San Remo, the 1925 revolt's British Palestine border context, and the 1941 British invasion (Operation Exporter) that re-opened independence.
- 1919 Congress explicitly preferred US → British assistance and rejected France outright — key evidence that anti-French feeling was paired with pro-British (conditional) orientation.
