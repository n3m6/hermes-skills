## Key verified facts (quick-reference)

- Simla Deputation: 1 Oct 1906, 35 leaders under Aga Khan III; address drafted by Syed Hussain Bilgrami, signed by 1,182; → Indian Councils Act 1909 separate electorates; → AIML founded Dhaka 30 Dec 1906. Wasti (1976) argues Muslim-originated, not British-engineered.
- Congress reading of 1909: "imperial attempt at control through an elective policy of divide-and-rule" (Banglapedia, quoted in Indian Councils Act article).
- Silk Letter: Mahmud Hasan left Bombay 18 Sep 1915 for Hejaz; arrested Dec 1916 by Sharif Husain of Mecca (British ally), handed to British; Fort Verdala, Malta; released May 1920; "Shaykh al-Hind" title from Khilafat Committee.
- Ubaidullah Sindhi: Kabul Oct 1915; Provisional Government of India founded 1 Dec 1915 (Mahendra Pratap president, Barkatullah PM, Sindhi Minister for India); dissolved 1919 under British pressure.
- Khilafat: committee founded 1919 (Lucknow HQ); Khilafat Manifesto 1920; London delegation 1920 (led by Muhammad Ali; British treated delegates as "quixotic pan-Islamists" — Minault); Gandhi-Congress alliance; suppressed late 1921; caliphate abolished 1924. Jinnah resigned from Congress 1920 over the religious entanglement.
- Jinnah: declined knighthood 1925 ("I prefer to be plain Mr Jinnah"); Simon boycott 1928; RTC delegate (1st & 2nd); London 1930–34 practising before Privy Council; told Viceroy 6 Feb 1940 League would demand partition; 11 Aug 1947 speech; died 11 Sep 1948.
- Lahore Resolution 23 Mar 1940: "independent states" (plural) for NW & E zones; moved by A.K. Fazlul Huq; rejected 1935 federation as "totally unsuited".
- Objectives Resolution: presented 7 Mar 1949, passed 12 Mar 1949; "Sovereignty over the entire Universe belongs to Allah Almighty alone"; 21 of 75 voted against, all 10 minority members; Maududi's divine-sovereignty theory seen as intellectual source.
- Maududi: JI founded Aug 1941; opposed partition (ummah doctrine); jailed 1948 (Kashmir war opposition), death sentence 1953 (anti-Ahmadi agitation, commuted).
- AIML growth: 400 members (1906) → 2 million (1943); won 425/476 Muslim seats in 1946 elections.
